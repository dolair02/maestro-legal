Maestro Customer Support & FAQ

Need help with your iPhone, iPad, or Mac piano setup? We respond within 24 hours at support@maestropiano.app.

How do I connect a MIDI keyboard?

Plug your USB-C or Lightning MIDI keyboard into your iPhone, iPad, or Mac (or pair via Bluetooth MIDI in Audio MIDI Setup) and tap Rescan MIDI inside Maestro. All CoreMIDI note and velocity events are recognized automatically.

How do I restore or manage my Maestro Pro subscription?

Open Maestro and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the membership screen. To change plans (Weekly $9.99, Monthly $19.99, 3-Month $39.99, 6-Month $59.99, or Yearly $99.99) or cancel auto-renewal, open Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your device.
