Privacy Policy — Maestro: AI Piano Coach & Studio

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

1. On-Device Audio & MIDI Processing

Maestro processes all microphone pitch detection and CoreMIDI keyboard events locally in real time on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac. We never record, store, or upload raw microphone audio to external servers.

2. Local Learning Progress

Your onboarding quiz answers, daily practice target, completed lessons, and score streaks are stored locally on your device. No third-party advertising trackers or cross-app behavioral profiles are used.

3. Subscriptions & Apple StoreKit 2

All auto-renewable subscriptions ($9.99/week, $19.99/month, $39.99/3 months, $59.99/6 months, and $99.99/year) are billed and managed directly by Apple via StoreKit 2. Maestro never accesses your payment card details.

4. Contact & Data Requests

If you have any questions about privacy, contact us at support@maestropiano.app.
